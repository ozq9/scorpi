The tour

01

Answers you can look at.

Ask something and Scorpi does the work: it searches, checks the weather, reads your files, then lays the answer out on a live canvas with charts, maps and cards. The short version is said out loud.

02

A team that works while you talk.

Bigger jobs go to agents. The Coder builds websites and apps in its own folder, the Researcher writes cited reports, and you can make your own: remind me every day at 13:00 to take a break.

03

Or just talk.

Switch to Voice and have a conversation. Scorpi answers in a natural voice, and you can interrupt it any time. It stops and listens.

04

Make it yours.

The Studio changes every colour, font and corner with a live preview. Or describe a look, warm paper with serif headings, and Scorpi makes it. Plugins add panes, commands and buttons.

05

In your pocket, too.

Open Scorpi on your phone over Wi-Fi or Tailscale and add it to your home screen. Your PC does the thinking; a pairing PIN keeps everyone else out.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Scorpi really free?

Yes. Scorpi is free, and so can everything it uses be: local models through Ollama, free cloud tiers, free neural voices, OpenStreetMap, Open-Meteo weather and DuckDuckGo search. Paid keys are optional.

Where do my chats and keys go?

They stay on your PC, in Scorpi's own data folder. Messages only go to the model provider you picked, or nowhere at all with a local model.

Do I need a powerful PC?

No. With a free cloud model any Windows 10 or 11 PC works. A strong GPU only matters if you want to run models offline.

Can it break things on my computer?

Reading is allowed by default. Running code, controlling the PC, deleting and sending things ask you first, and you can set every tool to Allow, Ask or Off.

How do I remove it?

Like any app: Settings → Apps → Scorpi → Uninstall. You choose whether your chats and settings stay for later.

Why does Windows warn me about the download?

Windows shows that for programs without a paid code-signing certificate. You can check the download against the SHA-256 at the bottom of this page.
